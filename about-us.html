About Us

Building better relationships between dogs and their owners.

Our Story

Pawsitive Obedience Academy was created to help dog owners understand and communicate more effectively with their dogs.

We believe that training should be a positive experience for both dogs and their owners. Our approach focuses on patience, consistency, communication and positive reinforcement.

Our Mission

To provide professional, accessible and positive dog-training services that improve communication and understanding between dogs and their owners.

Our Vision

To become a trusted canine training organisation recognised for effective training, professional service and positive outcomes.

Our Training Philosophy

We use positive reinforcement to encourage dogs to learn through rewarding desirable behaviour. Our goal is to create a supportive training environment where dogs can learn at their own pace.
